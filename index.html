About Me

I'm Ahmed Abualhamd — a video editor and motion designer working under the brand ABUALHAMD EDITS. I craft cinematic, rhythm-driven edits where every cut lands on the beat and every frame is built to hold attention.

My style is cinematic, premium and smooth: overlapping camera motion, match cuts and whip transitions, strong typography and layered sound design — heavy editing detail without losing clarity. I work remotely with clients worldwide, in Arabic and English.
